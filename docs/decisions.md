| D55 | Sending | Next-month invoices approved before the send day go out on the **28th at 09:00**; anything approved after that, and joining (part-month) invoices, go straight away. Approved invoices can go back to draft until sent. | Proposed (from D19, D24) |
| D56 | Refunds and credits | A refund request is raised automatically when an invoiced member day is cancelled free (48 h+ ahead, or by the Owner without charge). Late cancellations and no-shows are simply not refunded – no extra charge is needed as members pay in advance. | Proposed (from D18, D20) |
| D57 | Scheduled jobs | One idempotent “tick” (every 5–15 min): 01:00 book membership days ahead, 02:00 refresh drafts and catch missed refund requests, email the Owner once when next month’s drafts are ready, send approved invoices when due, send reminders at 15:30 on day 4. Each daily job runs once per day (job_runs); failures retry on the next tick. | Proposed |
| D58 | Refunds to card | Automatic (Stephen, 29 Sep 2026). Ad hoc / extra / trial days cancelled 48 h+ ahead (or by the Owner without a charge) are credited and refunded to the card straight away; member refunds go back the moment the Owner approves them; any credit on a card-paid invoice, and any overpayment, is refunded automatically. Stripe keeps its fee (D20). Failed refunds are listed under Refunds to try again; money paid another way is returned by hand. | Approved |
| D59 | Paying invoices and Owner bookings | Members pay each invoice with a **Pay by card** button (no stored cards). When the Owner books a priced day, the customer is emailed a payment link and the place is held for up to 24 hours (or until the session starts); unpaid places are released automatically. | Approved (Stephen, 29 Sep 2026) |
| D60 | Pay at booking (D6 in practice) | Customer bookings with a price are held for 31 minutes (Stripe’s minimum checkout time is 30) while they pay on Stripe’s page; held places count against capacity. On payment the places are confirmed and a paid invoice (same LK9DOUGIE- numbering) and receipt are issued. If a hold lapsed and the place went before the payment arrived, that day is credited and refunded automatically. Cancelling an unpaid booking releases it. Waitlist places accepted later are paid the same way; membership days stay on the monthly invoice. | Proposed |
| D61 | Card fees | Stripe UK: no monthly fee; 1.5% + 20p standard UK cards, 2.8% + 20p premium UK cards, 2.5% + 20p EEA, 3.15% + 20p international (stripe.com/gb/pricing, checked 29 Sep 2026). Absorbed by the business – UK law bans card surcharges to consumers. | Flagged to Owner (29 Sep 2026) |
